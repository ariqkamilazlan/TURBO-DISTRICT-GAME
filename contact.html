Game overview

TURBO DISTRICT is a stylized open-world 3D arcade racing game. You arrive in the district with one beaten-up Spark GT and a dream of owning the streets.

Synopsis: win street races to earn rep, unlock new cars and take on the mountain crews and highway legends. Every road is open from the start.

Game features

  • Open-world city, mountain and highway racing
  • Eight cars with their own look and handling
  • Arcade drifting, nitro boost and tuning
  • Stylized low-poly 3D world
