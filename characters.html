How the cars play

Drift

Hold the handbrake into a corner to slide through and build your boost.

Nitro boost

Fill the meter with clean drifts and near-misses, then burn it on the straights.

Tune and paint

Upgrade the engine and tyres, then pick a livery that makes your car yours.

Garage

Switch cars any time. AWD for grip, RWD for style and skill.
