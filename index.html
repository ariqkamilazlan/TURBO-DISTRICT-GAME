Game introduction

TURBO DISTRICT is a stylized open-world 3D arcade racing game. Cruise a neon city, attack twisting mountain roads and flat-out down endless highways with eight unique cars.

It is a city that never sleeps and never slows down. You arrive with one beaten-up Spark GT and a dream of owning the streets.

Win street races to earn rep, unlock new cars, and take on the mountain crews and highway legends. Every road is open from the start. Where you race, and how fast, is up to you.

  • 8 cars to collect
  • 3 open-world zones
  • 360 km/h top speed
